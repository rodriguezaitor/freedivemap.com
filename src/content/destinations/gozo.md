    ## About Gozo

    Gozo, the second largest island in the Maltese archipelago, offers exceptional freediving opportunities with access to the famous Blue Hole and other world-renowned dive sites in the Mediterranean.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    - **Visibility**: Typically 20-40 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Mild to warm (16-26°C depending on season)
    - **Conditions**: Generally calm Mediterranean waters
    - **Marine Life**: Diverse Mediterranean species including groupers, octopus, and colorful reef fish

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **Blue Hole**: World-famous natural arch and sinkhole
    - **Inland Sea**: Unique lagoon connected to the sea
    - **Xlendi Bay**: Protected bay perfect for training
    - **Dwejra Bay**: Area with multiple excellent dive sites

    ### Training Facilities

    Gozo offers several freediving schools with access to deep water sites and the famous Blue Hole, making it ideal for advanced training and competitive freediving.

    ### Sitios de Buceo Principales

    - **Blue Hole**: Arco natural y sumidero mundialmente famoso
    - **Inland Sea**: Laguna única conectada al mar
    - **Xlendi Bay**: Bahía protegida perfecta para entrenamiento
    - **Dwejra Bay**: Área con múltiples excelentes sitios de buceo

    ### Instalaciones de Entrenamiento

    Gozo ofrece varias escuelas de freediving con acceso a sitios de aguas profundas y el famoso Blue Hole, convirtiéndolo en ideal para entrenamiento avanzado y freediving competitivo.
