    ## About Dahab

    Dahab is one of the world's most famous freediving destinations, located on the Red Sea coast of Egypt. The town is renowned for the legendary Blue Hole, a 130-meter deep sinkhole that attracts freedivers from around the globe.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    The Red Sea offers exceptional conditions for freediving with:
    - **Visibility**: Often exceeds 30 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Warm year-round (22-28°C)
    - **Depth Training**: Excellent opportunities for deep training
    - **Currents**: Generally calm, perfect for beginners and advanced divers

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **The Blue Hole**: World-famous 130m deep sinkhole
    - **The Canyon**: Beautiful underwater canyon formation
    - **The Bells**: Popular training spot with easy access
    - **Lighthouse**: Shallow reef perfect for beginners

    ### Training Facilities

    Dahab hosts numerous world-class freediving schools offering courses from beginner to instructor level, including AIDA, PADI, and SSI certifications.

    El Mar Rojo ofrece condiciones excepcionales para freediving con:
    - **Visibilidad**: A menudo supera los 30 metros

    - **The Blue Hole**: Sumidero mundialmente famoso de 130m de profundidad
