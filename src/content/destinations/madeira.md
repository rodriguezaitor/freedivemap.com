    ## About Madeira

    Madeira, a Portuguese island in the Atlantic Ocean, offers excellent freediving opportunities with clear waters, diverse marine life, and beautiful underwater formations. The island is known for its year-round mild climate and excellent visibility.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    - **Visibility**: Typically 15-25 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Mild year-round (18-24°C)
    - **Conditions**: Generally calm, excellent visibility
    - **Marine Life**: Groupers, barracuda, colorful reef fish, and occasional dolphins

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **Garajau Marine Reserve**: Protected area with diverse marine life
    - **Cabo Girão**: Dramatic wall dive with excellent visibility
    - **Reis Magos**: Popular dive site with clear waters
    - **Ponta de São Lourenço**: Beautiful underwater formations

    ### Training Opportunities

    Madeira offers good conditions for freedivers of all levels. Several dive centers provide freediving courses and equipment rental in the beautiful Atlantic waters.

    - **Garajau Marine Reserve**: Área protegida con diversa vida marina
    - **Cabo Girão**: Buceo en pared dramática con excelente visibilidad
