    ## About Negril

    Negril, located on Jamaica's western coast, offers excellent freediving opportunities in the warm Caribbean waters. The area is known for its clear waters, beautiful coral reefs, and diverse marine life.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    - **Visibility**: Typically 15-25 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Warm year-round (26-29°C)
    - **Conditions**: Generally calm, excellent for freediving
    - **Marine Life**: Colorful reef fish, turtles, rays, and vibrant coral formations

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **Negril Reef**: Beautiful coral formations and clear waters
    - **Throne Room**: Popular dive site with excellent visibility
    - **Shallow Plane Wreck**: Accessible wreck dive
    - **Bloody Bay Wall**: Dramatic wall dive

    ### Training Opportunities

    Negril offers good conditions for freedivers of all levels. Several dive centers in the area provide freediving courses and equipment rental.
