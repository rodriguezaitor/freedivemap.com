    ## About Valletta

    Valletta, the capital of Malta, offers world-class freediving facilities with access to deep water sites in the Mediterranean. The area is known for its excellent training centers and professional freediving infrastructure.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    - **Visibility**: Typically 15-30 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Mild to warm (16-26°C depending on season)
    - **Conditions**: Generally calm Mediterranean waters
    - **Marine Life**: Diverse Mediterranean species including groupers, octopus, and colorful reef fish

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **Blue Grotto**: Famous cave system with excellent visibility
    - **Cirkewwa**: Deep water site popular with freedivers
    - **St. Paul's Bay**: Protected area perfect for training
    - **Comino**: Crystal clear waters between Malta and Gozo

    ### Training Facilities

    Malta offers several world-class freediving schools and training centers, with access to deep water sites and professional facilities ideal for competitive training and certification courses.
