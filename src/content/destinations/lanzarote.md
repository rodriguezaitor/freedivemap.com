    ## About Lanzarote

    Lanzarote, one of the Canary Islands, offers unique freediving experiences with its volcanic underwater landscapes. The island's Atlantic location provides excellent visibility and access to diverse marine ecosystems.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    - **Visibility**: Typically 20-35 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Mild year-round (18-23°C)
    - **Conditions**: Generally calm Atlantic waters
    - **Marine Life**: Diverse Atlantic species including rays, groupers, and colorful reef fish

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **Playa Chica**: Protected bay perfect for training
    - **Museo Atlántico**: Underwater sculpture museum
    - **El Bajón**: Popular dive site with excellent visibility
    - **La Graciosa**: Nearby island with pristine dive sites

    ### Training Opportunities

    Lanzarote offers several freediving schools with access to unique volcanic underwater formations and excellent training conditions year-round.
