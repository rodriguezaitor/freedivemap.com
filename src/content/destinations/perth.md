    ## About Perth

    Perth offers world-class freediving along Western Australia's coast, with excellent visibility and diverse marine ecosystems. The area is known for its pristine waters and abundant marine life.

    ### Freediving Conditions

    - **Visibility**: Often exceeds 20 meters
    - **Water Temperature**: Mild to warm (18-24°C depending on season)
    - **Conditions**: Generally calm, especially in protected bays
    - **Marine Life**: Dolphins, seals, colorful reef fish, and diverse marine ecosystems

    ### Key Dive Sites

    - **Rottnest Island**: Island with excellent dive sites
    - **Cottesloe Beach**: Popular area with good diving
    - **Fremantle**: Historic port area with dive sites
    - **Swan River**: Estuary with unique diving opportunities

    ### Training Opportunities

    Perth offers several freediving schools with access to excellent dive sites, making it an ideal destination for freediving training in Western Australia.

    - **Visibilidad**: A menudo supera los 20 metros
    - **Temperatura del Agua**: Suave a cálida (18-24°C dependiendo de la temporada)
    - **Condiciones**: Generalmente calmadas, especialmente en bahías protegidas
    - **Vida Marina**: Delfines, focas, peces de arrecife coloridos y diversos ecosistemas marinos

    ### Sitios de Buceo Principales

    - **Rottnest Island**: Isla con excelentes sitios de buceo
    - **Cottesloe Beach**: Área popular con buen buceo
    - **Fremantle**: Área de puerto histórico con sitios de buceo
    - **Swan River**: Estuario con oportunidades únicas de buceo

    ### Oportunidades de Entrenamiento

    Perth ofrece varias escuelas de freediving con acceso a excelentes sitios de buceo, convirtiéndolo en un destino ideal para entrenamiento de freediving en Australia Occidental.
